HOW TO RUN THE PROJECT (1-CLICK SETUP):
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1. OPEN ON YOUR LAPTOP / PC:
   👉 Just double-click on:  "CLICK-TO-START.bat"  (or "START.bat")
   
   It will automatically:
   - Free ports 8080 and 3000
   - Start the Java Spring Boot Backend
   - Start the React Frontend
   - Automatically open your browser to: http://localhost:3000

2. OPEN ON YOUR MOBILE PHONE:
   👉 Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi / Hotspot as your laptop.
   👉 Look at the terminal window after clicking "CLICK-TO-START.bat".
   👉 Open the shown MOBILE PHONE LINK (e.g., http://192.168.1.X:3000)
      in Google Chrome / Safari on your phone.

3. HOW TO STOP THE PORTAL:
   👉 Just double-click on:  "CLICK-TO-STOP.bat"
